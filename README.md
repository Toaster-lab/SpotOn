# SpotOn
We prevent fuck ups!

## The problem
Female astronauts are taking pills to switch off their menstruations. This raises the risk of blood cloths and expire on longer space missions. By switching of their periods, they miss a monthly health signal from their body which prevents them to detect diseases early.

## Our solution
We take our knowledge from Earth and take it into space. We learn your unique body's baseline, study your menstrual blood and make a risk assessment! 

## Future vision
* Fill in the gap of women's menstrual cycle.
* Focus on improving healthcare for women
* Translating molecular and physiological into meaningful insights!!
